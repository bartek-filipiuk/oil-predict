# Data sources research (2026-09-09)

## Daily wholesale (backbone) - Orlen, undocumented JSON API
- Current: https://tool.orlen.pl/api/wholesalefuelprices
- History: https://tool.orlen.pl/api/wholesalefuelprices/ByProduct?productId=41&from=2026-01-01&to=2026-09-09
- productId: 41 Pb95, 42 Pb98, 43 ON Ekodiesel, 44 ON Arctic, 46 Ekoterm. No LPG.
- Unit PLN/m3 net (excise + fuel charge included, VAT excluded). History back to 2004.
- Needs browser-like User-Agent + `Accept: application/json` (F5 WAF rejects curl UA).
- Mirror with Brent + USD/PLN already merged: https://cenypaliw.fyi/api/chart-data.json (daily from 2020)

## Crude / FX (all tested, free)
- FRED CSV, no key: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DCOILBRENTEU&cosd=2026-01-01 (also DCOILWTICO, DDFUELUSGULF). ~1 week lag.
- Yahoo chart (needs Mozilla User-Agent): https://query1.finance.yahoo.com/v8/finance/chart/BZ%3DF?range=ytd&interval=1d (CL=F, HO=F, PLN=X, EURPLN=X). Same-day data.
- NBP: https://api.nbp.pl/api/exchangerates/rates/a/usd/2026-01-01/2026-09-09/?format=json (limit 367 days per call).
- EIA v2 needs free key; RBRTE series. Stooq blocked. ICE Gasoil / ARA diesel: no free daily source found.

## Price formation (PL, 2026)
- Orlen wholesale ~ ARA product quote (USD/t) x USD/PLN + logistics, lag 1-5 business days, asymmetric.
- Wholesale -> retail: increases 1-3 days, decreases 3-7+ days ("rockets and feathers").
- Fixed components per 1000 l: excise 1529 (Pb95) / 1160 (ON); cut to 1239 / 880 from 28 Mar to 15 Jun.
  Fuel charge 210.29 / 453.52, emission charge 80. VAT 23%, cut to 8% 31 Mar - 30 Jun (CPN I) and 17 - 31 Aug (CPN II, VAT + max prices only,
  Dz.U. 2026 poz. 1095, M.P. 2026 poz. 798). Max prices 31 Mar - 30 Jun and 17 - 31 Aug, daily on gov.pl/web/energia. From 1 Sep 2026: VAT 23%, full excise, no caps.
- Long-run retail margin ~21-25 gr/l; Jan 2026 ~52-56 gr/l; near zero / negative in Mar and early Sep 2026.
